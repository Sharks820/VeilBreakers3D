TOP 10 SPECIFIC GAPS vs AAA RPGs

1 No gradients anywhere — Every AAA button uses top-to-bottom gradients. Your buttons are flat solid colors. This alone makes them look 10 years old.
2 No depth/shadow system — AAA UIs layer box-shadows, inner glows, and drop shadows. Your elements float flat on the screen with zero z-depth illusion.
3 Wrong button layout paradigm — Horizontal button rows are for web apps. Every AAA RPG (Diablo, Genshin, Elden Ring, FF) uses VERTICAL stacked buttons, usually right-of-center or lower-center.
4 Buttons are undersized — At 160×50px your buttons are toolbar-sized. AAA main menu buttons are hero elements — large, commanding, impossible to miss.
5 No idle animation on buttons — AAA buttons breathe, pulse, shimmer. Genshin's buttons have subtle border glow animation. Diablo's have smoldering effects. Yours are static until hovered.
6 Placeholder hero models — The magenta capsule on the character select screen instantly signals "prototype." This is the single biggest visual credibility killer.
7 No ornamental decoration — AAA dark fantasy UIs use decorative corner pieces, ornate dividers, filigree borders. Your panels are clean rectangles with 1-2px borders.
8 Typography lacks weight hierarchy — AAA uses dramatic size differences (hero title 48px+ vs body 14px). Your sizes are too uniform — 36px max for hero name is small for a centerpiece.
9 No ambient glow/emission effects — AAA panels glow subtly from within. Borders emit light. Icons have halos. Your panels are dark boxes with thin borders.
10 Carousel is text-based — Character selection carousels in AAA use hero portraits/busts with selection rings. Unicode arrows (◀ ▶) are placeholder-tier.
