Design decisions locked in:

TITLE SCREEN (from V4 — approved "looks so sick"):
• Looping video background (need seamless loop — no glitch)
• Demon with idle breathing animation + interactive layers
• VeilBreakers logo: clickable (pulse+smoke), more visible against bg
• Hide Veo watermark
• Audio: demon laughs, girl crying softly, ambient music fade-in
• Buttons: all same size, dark base, orange gradient on hover only, Cinzel font
• VFX: embers, ash, vignette, scanlines, heat haze

CHARACTER SELECT:
• Hero stage: Full spotlight, no champion on stage. Rotatable model with idle animation
• Stats: HP(red)/STAMINA(green)/ATK(orange)/DEF(blue) as bars — 2×2 grid
• Attributes: STR/DEX/CON/INT/WIS/CHA as bold numbers only (no bars, no modifiers)
• Champion monster: In panel with name+tags+description PLUS 3D model viewer filling remaining space (rotatable)
• Cards: 80×100px, per-hero colors, hover lift animation (kept)
• Bottom bar: Cards + embark aligned together
• HERO 1/4: Top center
• Embark: Breathing glow + shine sweep + hold-press brightens + plays character voice line on confirm
• Panel: Layered gradient bg, glowing top border, ornamental dividers, depth shadows
• Theme system: Per-hero colors shift everything (panel border, tabs, stat accents, card colors)

• Title: demon laughs (random), girl crying softly, ambient music fade
• CharSelect: hero voice line on embark confirm
• Need: AI-generated or placeholder audio assets
