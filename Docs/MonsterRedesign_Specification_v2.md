# VeilBreakers Monster Redesign Specification v2
## Brand Coherence & Balance Overhaul

---

## Executive Summary

This redesign addresses critical brand-lore mismatches, ensures balanced brand distribution, and creates cohesive skill sets that reinforce each monster's identity.

### Key Changes
- **7 monsters** receiving brand corrections
- **2 monsters** receiving new brands (previously NONE)
- **Brand distribution** balanced to 2-3 monsters per brand
- **Skill redesign** to match brand archetypes
- **New GRACE monster** introduced (replacing Corrodex's mismatched brand)

### 3. Corrodex ⚠️ BRAND CHANGE
**OLD:** GRACE (7) + SAVAGE (2) ❌
**NEW:** VENOM (4) + IRON (1) ✅
**New Name:** **VenomKnight** (keeps Corrodex as subtitle)
**Role:** Acid-armored tank

**Updated Lore:**
> "An ornate suit of knight's armor, but instead of a body, it contains only acidic sludge. The armor itself has become poisoned - every plate, every joint drips with concentrated venom. It remembers being a guardian. Now it guards nothing but its own toxic existence. The bubbling inside isn't speech - it's acid eating away at the last remnants of the knight who wore it."

**Why VENOM + IRON:**
- VENOM: Acid/poison theme, DoT focus

- Removed GRACE which made no sense for acid creature

**Skill Set:**

## Skill Balance Guidelines

### Cooldown Standards
| Skill Type | Cooldown | MP Cost |
|------------|----------|---------|
| Basic | 0s | 0 |
| Defend | 0s | 0-5 |
| Skill 1 | 4-6s | 8-12 |
| Skill 2 | 10-15s | 15-20 |
| Ultimate | 45-90s | 25-50 |

### Power Scaling by Tier
| Tier | Base Power | Growth | Role |
|------|------------|--------|------|
| 0 (Common) | 10-15 | 1.05-1.15 | Early game |
| 1 (Uncommon) | 15-20 | 1.15-1.25 | Mid game |
| 2 (Rare) | 20-25 | 1.25-1.35 | Late game |
| 3 (Epic+) | 25-35 | 1.35+ | Boss/champion |

### Status Effect Chances
| Effect Type | Chance | Duration |
|-------------|--------|----------|
| Minor (slow) | 60-80% | 2-3s |
| Moderate (poison) | 40-60% | 4-6s |
| Major (stun) | 20-40% | 1-2s |
| Ultimate | 100% | 3-5s |
